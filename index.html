How the image to ASCII converter works

    
Three steps, all local. Your browser samples the image on a canvas, maps luminance to characters, and redraws live as you tweak parameters.

[ why_local ]

    
Image to ASCII without uploading

    
Most online ASCII art generators ask you to send the file to their servers. Semaphore never does. Pixels are read on a local <canvas>; conversion, preview and export stay inside the tab. That makes this image to ASCII converter safe for private photos, work screenshots, and anything you would not drop into a random form.

    

      

        
$ no phone-home

        
Production ships Content-Security-Policy: connect-src 'none', so the page cannot open a fetch, XHR or WebSocket — even if a future bug tried. Open devtools and watch the network stay quiet after load.

Once the static files and font are cached, the image to ASCII pipeline keeps working without a network. Close the tab and nothing about your photo is left on a remote disk.

[ vs_upload_converters ]

    
Not another upload-based ASCII generator

    
Upload converters are fine for throwaway memes. They are the wrong tool for private photos, work screenshots, or anything you would not paste into a stranger's form.

    

      

        
01 your bytes stay put

        
Semaphore samples the image in-process. There is no upload API, no processing queue, and no "we delete after 24h" promise to trust.

Columns, brightness, contrast, invert and color re-run every change. You see the ASCII art update immediately instead of waiting on a job id.

      

      

        
03 verifiable policy

[ faq ]

    
Image to ASCII FAQ

    

      

        Do my images get uploaded?
        
Never — the image to ASCII conversion runs entirely in your browser on a local canvas. It even works offline once the page is loaded. Production CSP blocks any outbound connect.

      

      

        Is this ASCII art generator free?
        
Yes — free, no account, no watermark, no quota. Static files only.

      

      

        Which image formats can I convert to ASCII?
        
Anything your browser can decode: png, jpg, webp and gif (first frame). Drop it on the converter or pick a sample.

      

      

        How do I get sharper ASCII art from a photo?
        
Start from a high-contrast crop. Raise columns toward 160–200, nudge contrast up, and try the braille charset when you need photographic detail at the same width.
