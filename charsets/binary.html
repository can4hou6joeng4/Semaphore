Three steps means shape, not shade

The ramp is a space, 0 and 1. There is no fourth step, so a gradient has nowhere to go: shadow is the space, the middle is 0, the highlight is 1. What remains is the boundary between them.

Contrast decides where those two cuts fall. Invert swaps which digit carries the subject, so the picture changes rather than merely darkening. Both digits are 7-bit ASCII. The full set of ramps is on the home page.
